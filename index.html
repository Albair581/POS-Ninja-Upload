POS Ninja Scanner Typing Utility 掃瞄器打字工具

Generate a QR code, scan it with a 2D barcode scanner, and it types straight into POS Ninja — no on-screen keyboard needed. 生成二維條碼掃描器的 QR 碼，將文字或庫存項目導入 POS Ninja。

Encoded payload 加密

Item-import codes are prefixed and re-validated inside the app before anything is created, so scanning a random QR code from elsewhere can't affect your inventory. 庫存品項的 QR 碼在應用程式內被前綴和重新驗證，因此從其他地方掃描隨機 QR 碼不會影響您的庫存。
